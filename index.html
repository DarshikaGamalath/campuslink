WHO WE ARE

Connectivity That Brings Your Buildings Together

CampusLink provides wireless connectivity solutions for organisations that need to connect nearby buildings.

Our CampusLink service is designed to extend an existing network using outdoor point-to-point wireless equipment when the site conditions are suitable.

Each installation is planned around the customer's building layout, network requirements, security needs and budget.
